Waze Voice Studio 1.0.01 - Windows x64

This single setup program installs the app and automatically downloads and installs its dependencies. An internet connection is required; it is not an offline bundle.

Allow at least 30 GB free disk space for the separate Python runtimes, VoiceStudio, caches and AI models, plus space for your recordings. Setup can take a long time on slow connections.

Setup automatically prepares VoiceStudio and all models required by Waze Voice Studio, verifies model initialization, and skips components that already pass compatibility and health checks. Automatic AI review uses your own Codex login; existing logins are reused. Audio generation runs locally in VoiceStudio.

Python and media tools are kept private to this application. Microsoft's C++ runtime may request administrator approval. VoiceStudio is a separate application; uninstalling Waze Voice Studio will not remove it or your saved voices.

This is an independent community project, not affiliated with Waze or Google.
